StatusPulse support

Purchase support

For delivery, correction, payment, or refund questions, use the support contact shown on your Stripe receipt. Include the receipt or Checkout Session identifier. The response target is two business days.

Pre-sales and public issues

For a non-confidential coverage question or a public-site defect, open an issue in the StatusPulse repository. Do not include personal information, payment details, private dependency inventories, credentials, or security reports in a public issue.

Security reports

Do not publish an exploitable vulnerability. Use the private contact method associated with the seller on Stripe Checkout or the purchase receipt.
