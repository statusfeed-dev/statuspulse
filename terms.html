Service and scope

One purchase covers a brief for up to 20 named SaaS or cloud dependencies. The brief maps available official status, service-health, support, and policy sources; identifies evidence gaps and due-diligence questions; and includes minimal normalized incident facts and calculated metrics only for source-policy-enabled integrations or separately permitted data. It includes stated coverage limits, a print-ready report, and a supporting CSV.

StatusPulse will deliver the brief to the checkout email within two business days after a successful payment and receipt of a usable dependency list. If the requested vendors cannot support a useful brief, StatusPulse will offer a replacement scope or a full refund before work starts.
