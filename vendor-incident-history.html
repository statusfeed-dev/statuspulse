Vendor incident history for reviews and postmortems

Vendor reviews and postmortems often need a durable record of what a provider publicly reported, when it was reported, and how long it remained unresolved. StatusPulse preserves those official incident timelines in portable CSV and SQLite formats.

A provenance-first workflow

  1. Choose the provider and review period.
  2. Export the incident records with source, started_at, resolved_at, impact, and fetched_at.
  3. Retain the source URL and collection timestamp with the review packet.
  4. Compare incident frequency and declared duration only with the stated provider coverage window.
  5. Keep the evidence separate from independent uptime, SLA, security, or causal analysis.

What the dataset contains

StatusPulse collects official public status feeds from 11 infrastructure providers and normalizes incident timelines for cross-provider analysis. The free preview includes a current 100-row CSV sample. The paid release provides the current full CSV and SQLite snapshot.

Limitations

Public status pages are provider-authored disclosures, not independent measurements. Coverage windows and schemas vary. This dataset is not synthetic monitoring, an uptime guarantee, breach history, compliance evidence, a security score, or causal attribution.

Sources are official public status APIs. StatusPulse is not affiliated with any listed provider.
